# tidytv list: realme 4K Smart Google TV Stick (SkyworthDigital YYT, Amlogic, Google TV 14, ~2 GB RAM).
# Owner uses only Netflix, Hotstar, YouTube and Prime Video. Everything else that is safe to switch
# off is switched off. Based on the community list tested on the same hardware:
# github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv/examples/skyworth-4k-google-tv-stick (MIT), plus the owner's choices.
#
# DISABLED for the main user, never uninstalled. Undo everything with `restore`, or one package with:
#   adb shell pm enable --user 0 <package>
# Packages not present on a given stick are skipped automatically.
#
# Kept on purpose (not in this list): Netflix (com.netflix.ninja), Prime Video
# (com.amazon.amazonvideo.livingroom), Hotstar (in.startv.hotstar), YouTube (com.google.android.youtube.tv),
# Play Store, Play services, keyboard, remote and Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, settings, firmware updates (com.sdt.ota),
# Chromecast built-in (com.google.android.apps.mediashell), and the home screen until step 2.
