# Privacy policy for Tilt & Sort

_Last updated: 28 November 2026_

Tilt & Sort is a game that runs entirely on your device.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal
information or any other data about you.

- It has no accounts and asks for no name, email address or other details.
- It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.
- It does not use the internet at all, and does not request the permission to.
- It requests no Android permissions.

## What stays on your device

The app saves your game progress (which levels you have unlocked, your stars, your best
times and best score) and your two settings (sound and tilt sensitivity) in the app's own
private storage on your device. This never leaves the device through the app. If device
backup is switched on in your Android settings, Android may include this small file in
your own backup, under your Google account and Google's privacy policy.

The app reads the phone's tilt sensor (accelerometer) while you play, only to move the
marbles. The readings are used at that moment and are not recorded.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes everything it
has saved.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
